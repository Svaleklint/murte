Murte

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Murte is an iOS app for friends to share when they're available and see when their plans overlap. This policy explains what data Murte handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Murte is developed by Victor Svaleklint, Sweden, who is the data controller. Contact: svaleklint@hotmail.com.

What we collect

Account

Your email address and password, used to sign in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we can't read them.

Profile

The display name you choose, and a profile photo if you add one. You can also add favourite League roles.

Availability and friends

The time slots you add (date, time and activity, e.g. League of Legends or Beer), which friends you're connected with, and your personal invite code.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token for your device so we can tell you when a friend is free at the same time as you.

League of Legends data (optional)

Only if you enter your Riot ID. We then fetch from the official Riot Games API: your account identifier (PUUID), ranked tiers, top champion mastery and recent match history. Match records are public game data and include the champions, roles, results and Riot identifiers of all players in those matches, which we store to work out statistics and which matches you played together with friends. We don't show anything about players who haven't linked their own Riot ID, apart from champion names in matches you share.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your schedule, your friends, overlap notifications and League stats.
  • To show your name, photo, availability and League stats to the friends you've connected with in Murte. Nothing is public to people who aren't your friends.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Legal basis (GDPR): providing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b)); linking a Riot ID and enabling notifications are optional and based on your choice (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, authentication, file storage and server functions. Data is stored in the EU (Stockholm region).
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications.
  • Riot Games is the source of League of Legends data. Champion images are loaded from Riot's content servers.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Clearing your Riot ID removes your linked League account data. Cached public match records may remain without being linked to you.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app at any time. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account; this permanently removes your profile, photo, availability, friendships and linked League data. You can also contact us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to processing. You have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

Murte isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
